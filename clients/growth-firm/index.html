01Website — Technical health
The website runs on WordPress with WordPress page-builder plugin — convenient to use but known to slow down websites significantly.">Elementor 4.0.8, hosted at Vercel.">Afrihost. WordPress page-builder plugin — convenient to use but known to slow down websites significantly.">Elementor is a known contributor to poor Core Web Vitals — bloated JS, synchronous font loading, and uncompressed images compound the problem. The findings below are not edge cases. They are the reason only 2 of 8 pages appear in Google's index.
CriticalSitemap and robots.txt are not publicly accessible

A request to growthfirm.co.za/sitemap.xml and growthfirm.co.za/robots.txt returns a blocked response. Without a sitemap, Google cannot schedule full re-indexing. This is a direct cause of 6 out of 8 pages being absent from search results.

Impact

Google cannot find or index the service pages that should be driving enquiries. The business is invisible for every search term it should rank for.

CriticalBroken footer link on every page — /who-we-are/ returns 404

Every page on the site links to /who-we-are/ in the footer. That page returns a 404 on every load. Google crawls every footer link — this means Googlebot is hitting a dead URL on every single page it visits, damaging crawl budget and signalling an unreliable site.

Fix

Either create the page or redirect the URL. This is a 10-minute fix that should happen before anything else.

CriticalPrivacy Policy links to a null anchor — sitewide

Every page on the site has a Privacy Policy link in the footer. It links to # — it goes nowhere. Growth Firm advises SME clients on POPIA compliance. The firm's own Privacy Policy is missing. This is a direct regulatory exposure, not just a UX gap.

WarningMobile performance estimated below 50 — WordPress page-builder plugin — convenient to use but known to slow down websites significantly.">Elementor is the root cause

The homepage hero image (1440×810px PNG) is uncompressed and served without WebP conversion. On a South African 4G connection this alone pushes LCP beyond 4 seconds. Google Fonts load synchronously. The Maps iframe on the contact page has no lazy-load facade.

Root cause

WordPress page-builder plugin — convenient to use but known to slow down websites significantly.">Elementor's architecture is incompatible with modern Core Web Vitals targets. This is a structural problem that cannot be fully resolved without moving to a modern framework.

WarningSocial links and contact form token broken sitewide

Facebook and LinkedIn icons in the footer link to # — no social profiles are connected. The contact form renders a visible Δ character — a Contact Form 7 security token failing to render invisibly. Both are visible to every visitor.

02Website — Search & visibility
CriticalTwo service pages describe a completely different company — an energy firm

The Shared Service Centre and Access to Markets pages have this live meta description: "Synergy Trading & Projects provides innovative energy services, from solar power to energy efficiency solutions, for sustainable business operations."

This belongs to a different company entirely. The site was clearly built from a cloned template and this was never corrected. A corporate procurement team researching Growth Firm would see this. It should be corrected immediately.

CriticalEvery page shares an identical meta description

Every page on the site uses the same meta description. Google ignores duplicate meta descriptions and generates its own snippets — typically pulling the least relevant portion of the page. Growth Firm has zero control over how any page appears in search results.

CriticalNo Google Business Profile confirmed

No Google Business Profile was surfaced for Growth Firm in any search. The contact page references Cube Workspace Fourways — but that address has not been registered with Google. This is a near-zero cost, high-impact fix. Competitors in the Fourways compliance space (Evergreen Accounting, GAS Accounting, CAJPATS, Anthurico) all have active profiles and rank for local search terms Growth Firm should own.

WarningLocation absent from all on-page SEO — invisible for local search

Fourways and Gauteng do not appear in any H1, H2, page title, or meta description. Every competitor in this market explicitly targets Fourways, Sandton, and Northern Johannesburg in their headings. Growth Firm is invisible for all local search terms.

03Website — Analytics & tracking
This is the most operationally damaging finding in the entire website audit. Growth Firm is completely dark on its own digital performance.
CriticalNo GA4 tag present on any page — zero traffic visibility

No Google Analytics 4 tag, no Google Tag Manager container, no measurement ID — across every page. Growth Firm cannot answer: how many people visit the site, where they come from, what pages they view, whether anyone has submitted the contact form, or which channels drive traffic.

The consequence

There is no baseline. Without a baseline, any improvement to the site is unmeasurable. Installing GA4 is the first step before anything else is touched — it creates the evidence that justifies every subsequent decision.

CriticalNo Google Search Console — no index coverage data

No GSC verification tag detected. Without Search Console, Growth Firm has no access to keyword impressions, click data, crawl errors, or index coverage reports from Google. The 2-of-8 indexing figure in this report is based on a manual site: search — the actual picture may be worse.

04Website — Content & UX
CriticalNo named team members anywhere on the site

Growth Firm sells accounting, tax, payroll, and legal services — high-trust, high-stakes professional services. There is not a single person's name, photo, or individual credential on the site. The About page references SAICA, SAIT, IODSA, and LPC accreditations without naming who holds them. Anonymity is a conversion barrier in this category.

WarningAccreditation logos buried on the About page

SAICA, SAIT, IODSA, and LPC are significant credibility signals for this market. They are on a secondary page most visitors never reach. They should be on the homepage, visible without scrolling.

WarningNo pricing signal of any kind

Competitors in the Fourways market publish starting prices. CAJPATS lists R3,500/month for a bookkeeping package. Growth Firm offers no indication of cost — not a range, not a starting point. An SME owner comparing multiple firms will not submit an enquiry to a firm that gives no pricing signal at all.

05Portal — Brand & identity
The compliance portal at portal.growthiq.co.za was audited via authenticated access. The underlying data architecture is genuinely strong. The problem is not what the tool does — it is that almost none of it is accessible, discoverable, or presented in a way that builds client trust.
CriticalPortal runs on a completely different domain — no brand connection to Growth Firm

The portal operates on growthiq.co.za, not growthfirm.co.za. A client receiving a login link sees a URL that shares no brand equity with Growth Firm. To any outside observer, these appear to be two separate organisations.

The trust problem

The portal is not linked anywhere on the main site — no "Client Portal" button, no mention of it as a service feature. It is accessible only to people City has personally handed a link to.

CriticalPortal has zero pages in Google's index

A site:growthiq.co.za search returned no results. The domain does not exist as far as Google is concerned. Robots.txt is blocking all crawlers. Whether this is intentional or a misconfiguration needs to be confirmed.

06Portal — UX & navigation
CriticalDefault landing page is a raw admin user table, not the dashboard

When a user logs in, the portal lands on /Users/User — a raw admin user management table. The actual dashboard is at /Home. Every user's first experience of the portal is a system administration screen. This is a routing configuration change, not a rebuild.

CriticalCompliance tasks and documents are buried four clicks deep

The two things an SME client logs into the portal to check — their compliance tasks and their documents — have no top-level navigation entry. To reach compliance tasks: SME Management → find the company → open the record → click Action List tab. Documents follow the same four-step path.

The operational consequence

Corporate clients who log in cannot easily see the compliance status of their SME portfolio. Most will contact City directly instead. That is the work the portal was built to remove — and it remains manual because the UX buries it.

WarningSidebar collapses at 1440px — most laptops see icon-only navigation with no labels

The sidebar collapses to icon-only mode below approximately 1600px. The most common laptop resolutions (1366px and 1440px) fall below this threshold. No tooltips were confirmed on hover in collapsed state. Users on the most common hardware see a navigation strip with no labels.

WarningTest and development data is live in production

The Assessments list includes "GAP Assessment 2" (0 questions), "Test" (0 questions), and "new" (1 question) — placeholder records visible alongside live assessments. A developer test user email is active in User Management. If a corporate client ever views the Assessments list, this immediately undermines credibility.

07Portal — Technical findings
CriticalSentry error monitoring is broken — production errors are completely invisible

Both Sentry CDN scripts return HTTP 503. The monitoring tool built to catch production errors is itself broken. The version referenced (8.5.0) is outdated. The development team has zero visibility into errors occurring in the live portal.

What this tells us

A development team on an active retainer would have caught a broken monitoring script within days of go-live. The fact that it has gone unaddressed suggests no active maintenance is in place — a question we will need to confirm directly.

CriticalZero analytics inside the portal — no usage visibility

No GA4, GTM, or any analytics script was detected inside the portal. Growth Firm cannot answer: which of its 14 SMEs are actually logging in? Which corporate clients are engaging with their portfolio? Which compliance tasks are being completed?

Why this matters for scaling

Without usage data, managing 50 SMEs instead of 14 means proportionally more manual monitoring. The data that would enable Growth Firm to scale its compliance service does not exist yet.

WarningDocument expiry monitoring appears non-functional for past dates

A BEE certificate with an expiry date of November 2025 — six months ago — is on file. The dashboard shows "Documents Expiring This Month: 0" and "Documents Expiring Next Month: 0". An already-expired compliance document is not being flagged. The expiry monitoring system appears to only track future dates.

WarningSeveral visible content errors across the portal

Browser tab title reads "Growth Firm - Growth Firm" on every tab. Assessment edit page title reads "Edit - Assessmnet" — a typo on every assessment edit interaction. Dashboard widget reads "Total Number of SME's" — misplaced apostrophe on the primary KPI. These are minor individually; collectively they signal a portal that has not been reviewed since handover.

08Portal — What works
The portal investment is not wasted. Before discussing what needs fixing, this is what Netgen built correctly — and why remediation is worth pursuing rather than starting from scratch.
WorkingRich SME profile architecture — 10 tabs per client record

Each SME record has 10 tabs: Details, Funding, Employees, Documents, Action List, Client, Assessments, Features, Financials, and Operational Progress. This is a comprehensive data structure for managing compliance across a corporate SME portfolio. The architecture supports the service Growth Firm delivers — it needs the UX layer to surface it properly.

WorkingCompliance action tracking with deadline management

The Action List within each SME record tracks compliance tasks with category, assigned person, deadline, notes, and status badges (Overdue / In Progress / Completed / Not Started). Multiple tasks are confirmed as "Overdue" — the system works. It is buried, but it works.

WorkingAssessment infrastructure — scored questionnaires with categories and weighting

The assessment engine supports scored questionnaires, question categories and sub-categories, multiple answer support, and score weighting. A "Generate SME Journey Report" button exists. This is meaningful capability for a firm managing compliance programmes across a corporate client base.

WorkingAudit log, PWA infrastructure, and clean JavaScript at page load

The audit log records user, action type, system section, and timestamp — a compliance-ready paper trail. A Service Worker is registered, meaning PWA infrastructure exists. No JavaScript console errors were detected at page load — the application is technically stable at the foundation level.

09Questions we need answered
Before any migration or remediation work begins, these questions determine the scope, sequencing, and cost of everything that follows. Some can be confirmed in our first meeting. Others require access to hosting panels or a conversation with Netgen.
Question 01 — Hosting & access
Who currently manages the Vercel.">Afrihost hosting account for growthfirm.co.za — and does Growth Firm have direct login access to the Vercel.">Afrihost control panel?
This determines whether we can access the WordPress backend and migrate the site independently, or whether a third party needs to be involved first.
Question 02 — Netgen relationship
What is Growth Firm's current relationship with Netgen (Pty) Ltd — active support retainer, or build-and-handover with no ongoing engagement?
The broken Sentry monitoring and unresolved content errors suggest no active maintenance is in place — but this needs to be confirmed directly. The answer determines whether Netgen needs to be involved in portal changes, or whether we work independently.
Question 03 — Portal source code
Does Growth Firm hold the portal source code? Was a full codebase handover completed when the portal was delivered?
The portal is a NET/MVC application. If the source code was not handed over, configuration fixes require either Netgen's involvement or a front-end rebuild. This is a critical dependency for Phase 2 scoping.
Question 04 — Portal hosting
Is the portal hosted on the same Vercel.">Afrihost account as the website, or on a separate server? Who pays the hosting invoice?
Determines whether migration of the website and portal are one operation or two, and whether Growth Firm is managing both hosting costs directly.
Question 05 — Portal adoption
How do corporate clients currently interact with the portal — do they log in independently, or does Growth Firm generate and send reports on their behalf?
Zero analytics means we have no visibility into actual usage. If corporate clients are not logging in independently, the UX remediation priority shifts significantly.
Question 06 — Domain preference
Is there a preference to migrate the portal to portal.growthfirm.co.za, or is the growthiq.co.za domain intentional?
The brand split between the two domains is the single biggest trust problem for client-facing portal use. Consolidating to a subdomain of the main domain is straightforward — but requires a clear decision on whether growthiq.co.za has a separate intended purpose.
10Proposed way forward
Before any migration happens

The hosting migration is the right move — but migrating a broken site onto better infrastructure produces a fast broken site. The fixes below should happen before or alongside the migration, not after. Several of them (meta descriptions, Privacy Policy, broken links, Google Business Profile) take less than a day and have immediate impact on credibility and search visibility.

1
Immediate fixes — no rebuild required
Fix the /who-we-are/ 404. Replace the Synergy Trading meta descriptions on both service pages. Create a Privacy Policy page. Remove or connect the dead social media icons. Fix the contact form token rendering. Set up Google Search Console and submit a sitemap. Set up Google Business Profile for Cube Workspace Fourways. These items require access to the WordPress backend and an hour of focused work.
